REMOTE DESKTOP SHARING - INSTRUCTIONS
=====================================

LOCAL NETWORK USE:
1. Double-click START_REMOTEDSKTOP.sh on your desktop
2. Open your browser and go to: http://localhost:3000

INTERNET ACCESS (Works from anywhere):
1. Double-click START_INTERNET_ACCESS.sh on your desktop
2. Wait for the public URL to appear
3. Share that public URL with anyone, anywhere

SHARING YOUR SCREEN:
1. Open the URL (local or public) in your browser
2. Click "Share My Screen"
3. Grant screen sharing permissions when prompted
4. Share the Room ID with others who need help

VIEWING A REMOTE SCREEN:
1. Open the same URL in another browser/computer
2. Click "View Remote Screen"
3. Enter the Room ID you received
4. Wait for the host to approve your connection

HELPFUL TIPS:
- To test both sides on the same computer, use different browsers or Incognito mode
- The host must approve each viewer connection for security
- For Internet access, the free ngrok service may show a warning page first - just click "Visit Site"

STOPPING THE SERVER:
- For local access: Double-click STOP_REMOTEDSKTOP.sh
- For Internet access: Double-click STOP_INTERNET_ACCESS.sh
